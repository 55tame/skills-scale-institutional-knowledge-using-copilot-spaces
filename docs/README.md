# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's project-management guidance. The documents below explain the lifecycle, team responsibilities, and practices used to deliver and continuously improve projects.

## Project management process overview

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative lifecycle. **Initiation** validates the business need, defines measurable outcomes, identifies stakeholders, captures initial risks and dependencies, and decides whether work should proceed. **Planning** turns approved work into a prioritized and estimated backlog, sets acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, and maps dependencies, risks, milestones, and releases.

During **execution and tracking**, teams deliver small increments through a project board, communicate regularly, demonstrate progress, and monitor risks and blockers. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; the Product Manager owns outcomes and priorities; developers build and test; QA validates quality; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Blockers are escalated from the team through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor as needed.

For **release and deployment**, the team confirms acceptance criteria, passing CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback plans, then deploys, verifies, and communicates the release. **Retrospectives** after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents capture learning and turn it into a small set of owned, trackable improvements. Clear ownership, data-informed decisions, iterative delivery, and psychological safety apply throughout.
