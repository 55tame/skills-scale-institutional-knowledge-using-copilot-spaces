# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme project-management guidance. This README is the entry point to the Markdown process documents in [`docs/`](docs/).

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative lifecycle to move work from an idea to measurable outcomes:

1. **Initiation:** Validate the business need and desired outcome, identify stakeholders, define success criteria, capture initial risks and resource needs, and decide whether to proceed to planning.
2. **Planning:** Turn approved work into a prioritized, estimated backlog with clear acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done. Align on capacity, dependencies, risks, milestones, a release plan, and a test approach.
3. **Execution and tracking:** Deliver small increments using a project board, regular team and stakeholder communication, reviews and demos, automated tests, and ongoing progress and risk tracking. Escalate blockers from the team through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor when needed.
4. **Release and deployment:** Confirm acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and a rollback or mitigation plan. Deploy, verify the release, and communicate it to stakeholders and support.
5. **Retrospective and improvement:** After sprints, releases, milestones, and incidents, capture learnings, select a few owned action items, and track their completion and impact.

Throughout the lifecycle, the **Project Manager (PM)** coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications; the **Product Manager/Product Lead** defines outcomes and priorities; **developers** build, test, and review; **QA/testing** validates quality and acceptance criteria; and **stakeholders** provide input and approvals. The shared practices are clear ownership, customer value, data-informed decisions, iterative delivery, quality checks, transparent communication, and psychological safety so teams can learn and improve.
